Privacy Policy

How Remindra collects, uses, and protects your data.

Effective Date: April 4, 2026

Thank you for using Remindra. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data. By using Remindra, you agree to the terms outlined here. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary for Remindra to function. This includes: your name and email address (via Google Sign-In); the birthday entries you create (names, dates, optional birth years, notes, and reminder times); and notification data, including your Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token and notification preference. We also store language, country, timezone, and platform information with the notification registration.

If you choose a custom birthday notification sound on Android, Remindra stores the selected file reference and display name on that device so Android can play it for the reminder. The audio file and selection are not uploaded to Firebase or synced between devices. If you do not choose a sound, the app uses its bundled default sound.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Remindra service — specifically to store your birthday list, send you timely notifications, and personalise your in-app experience. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Auth), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. We apply industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

4. Third-Party Services

Remindra uses the following trusted third-party services: Google Firebase (authentication and database), Google Sign-In (account login), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (notification delivery). Remindra also schedules birthday alerts locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you have an active Remindra account or as required by law. To request account and data deletion, use Settings → Request data deletion in the app to prepare an email to support. Your data is not deleted until support has processed and confirmed your request.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To request account and data deletion, use Settings → Request data deletion in the Remindra app, or contact us directly at support.remindra@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Remindra is safe for users of all ages. The app contains no third-party advertisements, no pre-loaded content, and collects the minimal data described above. Users only see content they have created themselves. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective immediately upon posting. We recommend reviewing this page periodically. Continued use of Remindra after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support.remindra@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 3 business days.
