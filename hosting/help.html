❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I not receiving birthday notifications?
Make sure notifications are enabled in Settings → Notifications inside Remindra. Also check that your phone's battery saver or Do Not Disturb mode isn't blocking the app. On Android 13+, you may need to grant the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission manually in your phone's App Settings.
The notification sound isn't playing. How do I fix it?
Android permanently caches a notification channel's sound when the app is first installed. If you installed Remindra before the sound was added, you need to fully uninstall the app and reinstall it. This forces Android to create a fresh notification channel with the birthday sound attached.
Can I set different notification times for each birthday?
Yes! When you add or edit a birthday, tap the "Notification time" field to pick exactly when you want to be reminded for that specific person. Each birthday has its own independent notification time.
My notification was late. Why?
On some Android devices (especially Samsung), aggressive battery optimisation can delay alarms. Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Remindra → Battery → and set it to "Unrestricted". Also disable battery saver for the app.
How do I delete my account and all my data?
Open Remindra → Settings → Account → "Delete all my data". This will open a pre-filled email to our support team at support.remindra@gmail.com. We will delete your account and all associated data within 7 business days.
Will Remindra remind me in advance, not just on the day?
Yes. Remindra sends a push notification exactly 7 days before each birthday as a heads-up, plus a second notification on the birthday itself at the time you chose.
I forgot to sign in with Google. Can I use a different account?
Currently Remindra supports Google Sign-In only. Sign out from Settings and sign back in with any Google account. More login options are coming in a future update.
Is Remindra free?
Yes, Remindra is completely free. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges. We don't sell your data to third parties.
