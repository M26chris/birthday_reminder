Help & Support

Answers to common questions, and ways to reach us.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn birthday reminders on?
In Remindra, open the Settings tab, choose Reminders, and enable Remindra alerts. Allow notification permission when Android asks. If alerts are still blocked, check your phone’s Settings → Apps → Remindra → Notifications and battery settings.
Can I choose a different sound for a birthday?
Yes. On Android 8.0 or newer, add or edit a birthday, open The Sound, and choose an audio file available on your device. The choice is saved on that device and is not synced to your account. If you do not choose a sound, Remindra uses its default birthday sound. You can switch back to the default while editing the birthday.
Can I set different notification times for each birthday?
Yes. Add or edit the birthday and choose its reminder time under The Reminder. Each birthday has its own time.
Can I save a birth year or a personal note?
Yes. When you add or edit a birthday, the birth year is optional and you can add a note with details you want to remember, such as a gift idea.
My notification was late. Why?
Android battery management, Do Not Disturb, notification permission, or manufacturer settings can affect alert delivery. Check your phone’s Settings → Apps → Remindra → Notifications and Battery. Exact menu labels vary by device.
How do I delete my account and all my data?
Open the Settings tab and choose Request data deletion. Remindra opens an email to support.remindra@gmail.com with your account details. Support must process and confirm the request; requesting deletion does not delete data immediately.
When does a birthday reminder arrive?
Remindra schedules a reminder for the birthday at the time you chose. Delivery depends on notification permission and your Android device’s settings.
I forgot to sign in with Google. Can I use a different account?
Remindra currently uses Google Sign-In. Sign out from the Settings tab, then sign back in with the Google account you want to use. Your birthday list is associated with your Remindra account.
Is Remindra free?
Yes, Remindra is completely free. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges. We don't sell your data to third parties.
