About Remindra

Because every year counts.

Moments made timeless.

Remindra helps you keep the birthdays of your favourite people close. Save the dates, add personal notes and optional birth years, and choose when you would like a gentle reminder.

It is made to make remembering feel a little more thoughtful, so there is more time to celebrate the people who matter.
